# OpenSpool — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 June 2026_

**OpenSpool collects no personal data. None of your information ever leaves your device.**

OpenSpool is an offline line-capacity calculator for fishing reels. It is designed to work
entirely on your phone, with no account and no network connection.

## What we collect

Nothing. OpenSpool does **not** collect, store on a server, or transmit any personal
information. Specifically, OpenSpool has:

- **No account or sign-in.**
- **No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.**
- **No network requests** — the reel and line catalog is bundled with the app and used offline.

## Data stored on your device

The setups you save ("favorites"), any custom reels or lines you add, and your unit preference
(US / metric) are stored **locally on your device only**. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling
the app removes this data.

## Email

The app includes an optional email link in **Settings** so you can send corrections or feedback.
If you choose to email, your message is handled by your own email app and goes directly to the
developer; OpenSpool itself sends nothing automatically.

## Permissions

OpenSpool requests no sensitive permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, camera,
microphone, photos, or files.

## Children

OpenSpool does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new "last
