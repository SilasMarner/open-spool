## Full description (4000 char max)

```
Know exactly how much line your reel holds — before you spool up.

OpenSpool is a fast, offline line-capacity planner for saltwater anglers. Pick your reel, pick your line, and get the yardage in seconds — for a straight fill or a backing-plus-topshot mix. No account, no tracking, no ads.

What it does
• Straight fill — how many yards of any line your spool holds.
• Topshot — set your topshot length and the backing auto-fills the rest.
• Backing/Topshot — set either length and the other adjusts so the two fill the spool.
• Overflow warnings when a length won't fit.
• Save named favorites for the setups you run, and share a plan through any app.

How it works
Every reel is anchored to one manufacturer-published capacity, then any line is converted by the diameter-squared rule — the same physics tackle shops use. Accurate results come from good line diameters, and OpenSpool ships a growing catalog of real published specs.

Big built-in catalog
120+ reels and 280+ lines, including Avet, Penn, Shimano, Daiwa, Okuma, Accurate, Fin-Nor, Van Staal, Seigler, Maxel, Alutecnos, Abu Garcia, and Lew's — plus mono, fluorocarbon, and solid- and hollow-core braid from the major brands. Don't see your gear? Add a custom reel or line with one known spec and the app handles the rest.

Units your way
Switch between Standard (yd / lb / in) and Metric (m / kg / mm) — every number reformats instantly.

Results are close estimates — real fill depends on how evenly and tightly line is packed, so confirm the first time you spool up. Specs still awaiting manufacturer confirmation are flagged in-app.

Also from the developer: OpenTides
If you fish the tides, check out OpenTides — free tide and current predictions plus marine weather, in the same clean, offline-first, no-ads style. Search "OpenTides" on Google Play.

Tight lines!
```

## Notes
- Play listing descriptions can't contain clickable external links, so the OpenTides
  cross-promo is phrased as "search OpenTides on Google Play". The in-app Settings tile
  is the deep link (`https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mattbettinger.tides`).
- Title / short description are within Google's character limits.
